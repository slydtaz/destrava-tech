"Storage full" is the most annoying message on a phone: photos do not save, apps do not update, and everything slows down. You can free up gigabytes without losing anything important if you follow the right order. This guide works for Android and iPhone (menu names vary slightly by model and version).

## First: the safety rule

**Never delete anything before checking your backup.** Photos and chats are the most painful losses. So step zero is:

1. **Photos**: turn on backup in Google Photos (Android) or iCloud Photos (iPhone), or copy the photos to a computer/external drive. Check by opening a few photos already in the backup.
2. **WhatsApp**: turn on chat backup (Settings → Chats → Chat backup). Ideally encrypted — see our WhatsApp privacy guide.

Backup checked? Now you can clean up.

## Step 1: find out what is taking up space

- **Android**: Settings → **Storage** (or "Device care"). It shows the breakdown by category: apps, photos, videos, audio, downloads.
- **iPhone**: Settings → General → **iPhone Storage**. The colored bar shows the biggest item, and the list below sorts apps by size.

Look at the numbers before acting. The usual biggest items are: **WhatsApp, photos/videos, and forgotten apps**.

## Step 2: WhatsApp (usually the biggest item)

WhatsApp keeps every photo, video, and audio you receive — including those "good morning" chains from 2019.

1. In WhatsApp: Settings → **Storage and data** → **Manage storage**.
2. It shows chats sorted by size. Open the largest ones (family groups often lead).
3. Review and delete **videos and large files** — videos are 10 times larger than photos.
4. Preventive tip: in Settings → Storage and data, turn off **automatic media download** on mobile data and review the Wi-Fi setting. Less junk coming in means less cleaning later.

Text messages stay; you delete only the media. Anything in the backup can be restored.

## Step 3: photos and videos (with backup checked!)

- Delete **duplicates, old screenshots, and long videos** you never rewatch. Google Photos suggests items to free up space; on iPhone, Storage settings show "Review Personal Videos".
- Empty the **trash** afterwards (deleted photos take up space for 30 days).
- Screen recordings and old class recordings: move them to a computer before deleting.

## Step 4: apps you do not use

- **Android**: Settings → Apps → sort by size. Uninstall what you have not opened in months. Old games often take several GB.
- **iPhone**: in the Storage list, tap the app → **Delete App**. If you want to keep the data for later, use "Offload App" (removes the app, keeps documents and data).

An uninstalled app can be reinstalled for free. Your account data (online games, banks) stays on servers, not in the app.

## Step 5: cache and forgotten downloads

- **Cache** (Android): Settings → Apps → choose a large app (browser, social network) → Storage → **Clear cache**. Cache is a temporary file: you can clear it safely. **Do not tap "Clear data"** without knowing what it does — it logs you out and erases app settings.
- **Downloads**: open the Files app and review the Downloads folder. Installers (.apk), old PDFs, and downloaded videos are dead weight.
- **iPhone**: there is no manual per-app cache clearing; the system handles it. Reinstalling a very bloated app (for example, a social network) clears its cache.

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Install "miracle memory cleaner" apps — most are advertising, and some are malware. The system's own tools are enough.
- ❌ Delete strange folders in your file manager without knowing what they are.
- ❌ Trust "RAM boosters" — Android and iOS manage memory on their own.

## 5 minutes of maintenance per month

Once a month: review WhatsApp's Manage storage, delete old screenshots, and uninstall an app you do not use. A monthly check avoids a big cleanup later.

If space still runs out often, the problem may be the phone's storage size — then consider a model with more storage or a memory card (when the phone accepts one). In most cases, the steps above solve it.
