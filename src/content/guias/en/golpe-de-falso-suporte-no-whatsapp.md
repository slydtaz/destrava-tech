A message from someone claiming to be support may ask for data, money, or the installation of an app. The same approach arrives as an acquaintance who changed their number. Before replying, find out who is on the other side.

## Signs of a suspicious message

- a link you did not expect, especially to a login or payment page;
- a request to download an app outside the official store;
- a request for personal or bank data, such as a document, account, or card;
- a request for money, even in a message from a known contact;
- someone claiming to be an acquaintance writing from a new number.

## Confirm who is writing

If the person claims to be support or an acquaintance on a new number, call by voice or video using the number you already have in your contacts. Use the saved contact, not the number that arrived in the message. If the person does not answer or the story changes, stop there. Confirmation does not need to happen through a message with a link.

## Block and report

1. Open the chat and tap the name or number at the top.
2. Scroll to "Block" and confirm. The "Report" option is on the same screen; on WhatsApp Web, use the three dots of the chat.
3. When you report, WhatsApp receives the recent chat history and information about the reported account.
4. If the message came from a contact, warn the person by phone: their account may be in use by someone else.

## Protect your account

- Turn on two-step verification in Settings → Account → Two-step verification. The PIN is required when installing the account on another device.
- Set up a passkey if the app offers it. It gives you another way to sign in without relying only on the code sent by SMS.
- Set an app password and lock chats you want to keep out of the main list, if those options are available on your phone.
- If you store history in a cloud service, use encrypted backup with a password.

## If something goes wrong

- **You clicked the link or installed the app:** leave the page, uninstall the app, and report the chat. If you typed a password on a site, change it through official channels.
- **You gave bank data:** talk to your bank using the number on the back of your card or the official app and ask for whatever blocking is needed.
- **Your account was broken into:** reinstall WhatsApp and request a new code. Once you sign in, turn on two-step verification and tell your contacts through another channel.
