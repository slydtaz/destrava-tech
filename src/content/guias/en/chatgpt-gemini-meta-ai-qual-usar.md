ChatGPT, Gemini, and Meta AI: all three have a free plan, answer in Portuguese, and seem to do the same thing. So which one to use? The honest answer: **it depends on the task** — and none is "the best" at everything. This guide compares them practically, without favoring any of them.

> Note: these apps change almost every week. This comparison describes the general behavior of the free versions in November 2026; specific features may change. Test them yourself — it is free.

## Quick overview

| | ChatGPT (OpenAI) | Gemini (Google) | Meta AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where to use it | App and website | App, website, and built into Android/Google | Inside WhatsApp/Instagram and website |
| General strength | Text, reasoning, and programming | Integration with the Google ecosystem | Convenience: already in your WhatsApp |
| Initial cost | Free (with limits) | Free (with limits) | Free |
| Account needed | Yes | Yes (Google account) | No (uses your WhatsApp) |

## Where each one tends to stand out

### ChatGPT: long texts and structured reasoning

- Good results for **writing, text review, brainstorming, and explaining concepts** in depth.
- Strong in **programming** and in following complex instructions ("answer in this format, with these rules").
- The free plan has usage and feature limits; at peak times it may get slower or restricted.

### Gemini: for people who live in Google's world

- Natural integration with **Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Android** — useful for summarizing documents and working with your files (check permissions before connecting).
- Good for **research with current context** when connected to Search.
- If you already have a Google account (almost every Brazilian with Android does), there is no barrier to entry.

### Meta AI: the closest to your hand

- It is **inside WhatsApp** — no new app, no new account. For quick questions in the middle of a chat, nothing is more convenient.
- Generates images and answers everyday questions well.
- In groups, you can call it with `@Meta` and everyone sees the answer.
- We show the step-by-step in our Meta AI on WhatsApp guide.

## What all three do poorly (important!)

1. **They invent facts with confidence.** All of them. For dates, laws, formulas, news, and health, check an official source. AI is a smart draft, not an encyclopedia.
2. **Exact math and long calculations** may be wrong — ask for the steps and check them.
3. **They do not know you.** They have no access to your life, your files, or your private chats (unless you connect or paste them).
4. **Privacy has a cost.** What you write goes to the company's servers. Never send passwords, documents, bank data, or intimate information about other people.

## How to choose in practice

- **Quick question in the middle of a chat?** Meta AI, on WhatsApp.
- **Important text, deep study, or code?** ChatGPT.
- **Work with your documents and Google services?** Gemini.
- **Still unsure? Try the 3-minute test:** send the same question to all three and compare. You will quickly feel which one answers your kind of task better.

## Tips that apply to all of them

- **Give context**: who you are, what you want, and in what format. "Summarize in 5 points for a 15-year-old student" works better than "summarize".
- **Ask them to show their reasoning** and check the steps.
- **Iterate**: the first answer is a draft. "Improve the tone", "make it shorter", "give an example" — keep the conversation going.
- **Use AI to think, not to hand in finished work** — especially for school assignments. Our AI for studying without cheating guide shows the method.

## Summary

There is no universally best AI: there is the best one for the task at hand. Start with the one closest to you (Meta AI on WhatsApp), try the others when you need more depth, and check the answer before using it.
