Meta AI is Meta's artificial intelligence (the company that owns WhatsApp) and it chats with you like a contact. You can ask questions, request summaries, generate images, and plan things — without installing anything new. A study by Opinion Box/Mobile Time found that 7 in 10 Brazilians already use WhatsApp to get answers; Meta AI takes this further. There are important privacy precautions. Let's go step by step.

## How to call Meta AI

> Details may vary by app version and region. Keep WhatsApp updated through the official store (Google Play or App Store).

- **Its own chat**: in the chat list, look for the colored circle for Meta AI or search "Meta AI" in the search bar. Tap and chat as in a normal conversation.
- **Inside a group or chat**: type `@Meta` and select Meta AI to ask a question without leaving the conversation. Everyone in the group sees the question and the answer.
- **In the search bar**: in some versions, you can ask directly in the app's search.

If it does not appear for you: update the app, check whether the feature is available in your region, and restart WhatsApp. Availability changes over time.

## What it does well (practical examples)

- **Explain something in simple language**: "Explain compound interest as if I were 12 years old."
- **Summarize and organize**: "Summarize this text in 5 points" (paste the text — see the precautions below).
- **Plan**: "Make a study plan of 1 hour a day for the Enem in 3 months."
- **Write better**: "Review this message to make it more polite and clear."
- **Generate images**: "Create an image of an astronaut cat in watercolor."
- **Translate and practice languages**: "Talk to me in basic English and correct my mistakes."

Tip: **give context**. Instead of "how do I save money?", ask "I earn R$ 1.500 a month and spend R$ 400 on transport; how should I organize the rest?". The more context, the better the answer.

## What it is NOT

- **It is not a source of truth.** AI can invent information with complete confidence (a "delusion" or hallucination). For health, law, money, and important dates, **check an official source** before acting.
- **It does not know your life.** It cannot see your private chats, your contacts, or your files — it only responds to what you write to it.
- **It does not replace a professional.** A diagnosis, a court case, and serious investing call for a qualified human.

## Privacy: what never to share

When you write to Meta AI, your message leaves the end-to-end encryption of regular chats and is processed by Meta's systems to generate the reply. Treat it as a conversation with a service, not a private diary:

- **Never send**: passwords, verification codes, CPF, card numbers, bank data, documents, or photos of documents.
- **Avoid**: intimate information about yourself or other people (health, address, children's routine).
- **Careful with third parties**: do not paste other people's conversations or colleagues' data without a reason.
- In groups, remember that **everyone sees** what you ask Meta AI.

If you want to erase the chat history with Meta AI, delete the chat like any other — and check the app's privacy settings to understand what is stored.

Meta AI can help with everyday questions, studying, and texts, as long as you follow two rules: **check important facts in an official source** and **never share sensitive data**.
