Before traveling, decide where your phone's internet comes from. The options are roaming with your current carrier, a travel pass, or an eSIM from a local carrier or global provider. Each one has its own way of being purchased and activated.

## Check the device and the plan

1. Check whether your iPhone accepts a SIM card from another carrier. In Settings → General → About, look for the carrier lock field. If it says there is no SIM restriction, the phone is unlocked.
2. Confirm that the model supports eSIM. Compatible devices show an EID number in the device information, on the same Settings → General → About screen.
3. Ask your carrier whether your plan already includes data abroad and how much a travel pass costs. Write down the validity and the data amount before leaving the country.

## Choose where the internet comes from

- **Roaming with your current carrier:** you keep the same number. It works when your plan already includes data at the destination or when the travel pass costs less than a local SIM card.
- **eSIM from a local carrier or global provider:** you purchase it before the trip or already at the destination and activate it without swapping the physical SIM. It requires an unlocked device that supports eSIM.

## Activate the eSIM

Once you receive the eSIM, follow the method the carrier sent. Activation can happen by transfer from another iPhone, directly with the carrier (in some cases, through its app), by QR code, by link, or by manual entry of the details.

1. Open Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM (in some versions, "Add Cellular Plan").
2. Choose the method you received: scan the QR code, open the link, follow the carrier's activation, or type the details manually. To transfer from another iPhone, use the transfer option on both devices.
3. Go through the screens until the carrier confirms the plan is active.
4. Give the line a name, such as "Travel", so you do not confuse it with your Brazil line.

## Choose the line for data

With two active lines, the iPhone needs to know which one will use the internet. In Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data, select the travel line. In the same area, turn off mobile data for your Brazil line if you do not want to pay roaming. Also check which line is being used for calls in case you need to make calls at the destination.

## When you return

Turn off the travel eSIM when you get back. In Settings → Cellular, tap the travel line and turn off the plan. Select your Brazil line again in Cellular Data. If the eSIM has a short validity, you can also remove the plan on the same line screen.

## If something goes wrong

- **The eSIM does not activate:** check the device unlock, the validity of the QR code or link, and the details you typed. If the code expired, ask the carrier for another one.
- **No internet at the destination:** check that the travel line is selected in Cellular Data and that mobile data is on.
- **Unexpected charge on your Brazil line:** turn off mobile data for that line and confirm with the carrier whether roaming was active.
