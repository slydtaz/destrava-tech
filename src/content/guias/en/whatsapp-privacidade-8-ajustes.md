WhatsApp comes with settings designed for convenience, not privacy. Five minutes of changes reduce your exposure to scams, spam, and strangers. This guide covers the eight settings that matter most — all under **Settings → Privacy** (names may vary slightly by version).

## 1. Profile photo: contacts only

Scammers copy profile photos to create the "fake relative". If your photo is public, any stranger can download it.

**Setting:** Privacy → Profile photo → **My contacts**.

Also: use a photo that does not show documents, a name badge, a uniform with a name, or a car plate.

## 2. Last seen and online: who can see it

Knowing when you are online helps scammers choose the moment to strike ("I saw you were online, it's urgent").

**Setting:** Privacy → Last seen and online → **My contacts** or **Nobody**.

Note: if you hide yours, you will not see other people's either — that is the trade-off.

## 3. Groups: who can add you

Being added to "investment" or "promotion" groups is an entry point for spam and scams.

**Setting:** Privacy → Groups → **My contacts** (or "My contacts except..." to exclude specific people).

People who are not your contacts will need to send you a private invitation — you decide whether to join.

## 4. Calls from unknown numbers: silence them

Calls from unknown international numbers are usually a scam or spam.

**Setting:** Privacy → Calls → turn on **Silence unknown callers**.

They will not ring, but they stay in your call log if you want to check them later.

## 5. Disappearing messages: the default you forget to set

Messages that disappear on their own reduce what is exposed if your phone is lost or your account is stolen.

**Setting:** Privacy → Disappearing messages → choose a default (for example, 7 or 90 days) for **new chats**. Important chats can be adjusted individually.

Note: this does not prevent screenshots or forwarding — it is an extra layer, not absolute secrecy.

## 6. Read receipts: the two blue ticks

Less about security, more about peace of mind: turning them off avoids the pressure of "seen but no reply".

**Setting:** Privacy → turn off **Read receipts**.

Remember: it works both ways — you also stop seeing other people's blue ticks (in groups, receipts still appear).

## 7. End-to-end encrypted backup

By default, the backup of your chats on Google Drive/iCloud does **not** have the same encryption as messages. Anyone who accesses your Drive can read everything.

**Setting:** Settings → Chats → Chat backup → **End-to-end encrypted backup** → Turn on. Create a password or a 64-digit key and **keep it in a safe place** (without it, the backup cannot be restored).

## 8. Two-step verification (the most important one)

It is not exactly a privacy setting, but it is what stops someone from stealing the whole account — the most damaging WhatsApp scam.

**Setting:** Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Turn on, with a 6-digit PIN **and a recovery email**.

Full steps and explanations are in our two-step verification guide.

## Final checklist

- [ ] Profile photo visible only to contacts
- [ ] Last seen/online restricted
- [ ] Groups: only contacts can add you
- [ ] Calls from unknown numbers silenced
- [ ] Default disappearing messages turned on
- [ ] Read receipts set as you prefer
- [ ] Encrypted backup (password saved!)
- [ ] Two-step verification with PIN + email

Do it once and review every few months.
