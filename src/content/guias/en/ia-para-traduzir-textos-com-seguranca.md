Google Translate translates text you type or paste, in the app and in the browser. The result depends on the context you give, and the service is part of a Google account. It is worth knowing how activity is stored before pasting any text.

## Translating a text

1. Open Google Translate in your browser or the app and choose the text option.
2. Set the source language and the target language.
3. Type or paste the text in the left field and read the result in the right field.

Paste complete sentences instead of single words. A whole sentence shows the translator the subject and the meaning of the word; an isolated word leaves that information out. If the result sounds strange, add the previous or the next sentence.

## Checking the result

The result can be read aloud, shared with another app, or reversed. Reversing translates the translation back into the original language and helps you notice whether the meaning changed. Before sharing, read what will leave Translate, because the text passes to the app you choose.

## What is saved

Translate is one of the services in a Google account. Saved activity can be reviewed and deleted in your account settings. On a shared computer, activity may also stay in the browser history: sign out of your account and clear the history when you finish.

The pages consulted explain these controls and the translation steps. They do not decide what counts as sensitive data: remove from the text anything that does not need translating, such as document numbers, passwords, or other people's data.

## If something goes wrong

- **Translation makes no sense:** paste the whole paragraph, check the languages selected, and try again.
- **You want to erase what you translated:** open your Google account activity, filter by the service, and delete the records. In the browser, also clear the session history.
- **The text does not fit in the field:** translate it in parts and check the connection between them afterwards.
